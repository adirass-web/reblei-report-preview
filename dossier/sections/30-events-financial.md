## 4. Investment-management events

### Scout's Nasdaq settlement

Nasdaq accepted Scout Trading LLC's Letter of Acceptance, Waiver and Consent (AWC), case 20100243866-01, on April 7, 2015. Scout, CRD 151990, consented to a censure and a $3,000,000 fine without admitting or denying the findings, before a hearing and without adjudication. Michael Bleich was the managing-member addressee and signed for Scout. The respondent and sanctioned party was the firm; this instrument imposed no separate personal sanction on him. [[EVT-C001]] [[EVT-C002]]

The AWC describes a recurring exchange-traded fund (ETF) redemption and short-sale strategy that caused failures to deliver shares on time. According to its settlement findings, Scout submitted redemption orders while insufficiently long the required shares, used the T+6 close-out timetable for bona fide market making although the activity did not qualify, and repeated the transactions to maintain short positions. The findings identify Regulation SHO Rule 204 and Nasdaq Rule 2110 violations, together with inadequate supervisory systems and written procedures under Nasdaq Rules 3010 and 2110. These are findings accepted through settlement, rather than findings reached after a contested hearing. [[EVT-C004]] [[EVT-C002]]

The investigative period ran from January 1, 2010 through March 16, 2012. The narrower applicable period ran from November 9, 2011 through March 16, 2012, reflecting Scout's Nasdaq membership. The broader trading narrative provides context; the two periods cannot be treated as interchangeable dates for the action's coverage. [[EVT-C003]]
