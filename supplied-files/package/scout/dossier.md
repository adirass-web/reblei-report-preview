# Scout Trading and Michael Edward Bleich

Supplied-files diligence dossier • historical evidence, with source-specific limits

## Executive findings

**The strongest adverse evidence is a firm regulatory settlement.** Nasdaq accepted AWC 20100243866-01 on April 7, 2015 against Scout Trading, LLC, CRD 151990. Scout consented, without admitting or denying the findings and without adjudication, to findings concerning Regulation SHO Rule 204 and supervision, a censure, and a $3,000,000 fine. Michael Bleich signed as Managing Member for Scout; the supplied order does not name him as an individual respondent. The fine and payment election establish a sanction and an intended payment method, not collection, completion, a current debt balance or a personal fine. [Matter](record:prof-matter-nasdaq), [representative capacity](record:prof-scout-awc-signature), [fine finding](record:fin-scout-finding-fine-election).

**A substantial historical management and ownership connection is documented, but percentages remain bands.** BrokerCheck identifies Michael Edward Bleich, CRD 4262671, as Scout CEO and Managing Member from May 2009. Finch is reported as Scout's wholly owning parent; the indirect entries involve MEB NY Inc and NAYS LLC. These records support historical control and layered interests, without allocating firm assets, profits, distributions or unnamed-member balances to Bleich. [Role](record:prof-scout-ceo), [reorganization](record:prof-scout-reorganization), [indirect band](record:prof-scout-michael-meb-band), [allocation limit](record:fin-scout-finding-aggregate-capital-transactions).

**The financial reconstruction is a firm-level record, not a personal wealth estimate.** All five supplied 2009–2013 balance sheets reconcile at reported dollar precision. Gross assets were $155,680,893 at December 31, 2011 and $82,198,774 at December 31, 2013; members' capital was $8,748,360 and $6,501,113 respectively. Available 2010 and 2011 operations report profits while operating cash flow is negative. The missing 2012/2013 flow statements prevent reconstruction of annual profit, distributions and complete debt or cash movements. [Balance tests](record:fin-scout-finding-balance-snapshots), [2011 source](doc:cedbcd5bf0c7a6907fc2a5c30dde40f54259bdcfa46ab8db434f3b283050bbb9#page=6), [2013 source](doc:2b95a042ab5e1201576f72dd6c78bbbe91f21402768475d15f1868f160e9e7b9#page=6), [cash finding](record:fin-scout-finding-cashflow-source-ties), [coverage gap](record:fin-scout-finding-missing-flow-statements).

**Secondary material requires separate treatment.** The supplied advocacy report and research note contain biographical, later-career, offshore, litigation and disclosure theories. Their presence is evidence of what those authors asserted; it does not establish the alleged misconduct or current employment. The mixed records [prof-secondary-current-role](record:prof-secondary-current-role) and [prof-regulatory-litigation-unresolved](record:prof-regulatory-litigation-unresolved) are deferred scope boundaries only and supply no combined finding here.

The exact CRD 4262671 connects the regulatory Michael Edward Bleich in Scout BrokerCheck with the Michael Edward Bleich disclosed in Ophir's supplied ADV. The indirect entry reports Bleich through MEB NY Inc in the **75% or more** band from May 2009; this is an interest at an intervening level, not an exact ultimate Scout percentage.
