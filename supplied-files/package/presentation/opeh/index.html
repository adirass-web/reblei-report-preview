Executive findings

The supplied instrument documents one $2,233,007 principal with the source-named Michael E. Bleich and Alexis K. Bleich as personal makers and OPEH LLC as holder. The two makers, duplicate note files and repeated exhibits do not create separate principal amounts or establish equal shares. The note connects its tender to satisfaction and discharge of an Ophir Partners LLC corporate obligation in consideration of the makers’ personal guaranty. The predecessor agreement, standalone guaranty and cash-funding trail are absent; neither an additional corporate principal nor a new personal cash advance is independently established. One-principal finding; note, page 1, predecessor recital, page 3.

OPEH sought $2,233,007 principal, $155,070 accrued interest, further interest from September 17, 2018 at 15%/$930.42 daily, and unquantified fees and costs. The base addition and cent-rounded per diem reconcile. The underlying interest period, rounding convention and operative default date remain unresolved. The arithmetic scenarios below explain the sensitivity without selecting a legally controlling method. Component checks, interest boundaries, calendar finding; motion, page 1.

The action ended by a notice of discontinuance without prejudice and without costs; the order marked the motion moot and case disposed. This is the supplied procedural result. The requested judgment was not a money award, and discontinuance does not establish payment, settlement consideration, release or a later balance. A December 2020 administrative mailing was expressly corrected as inadvertently sent to disposed matters. Disposition, administrative correction; order, notice, correction.

These findings have high confidence as characterizations of the supplied text and reproducible arithmetic. Confidence does not authenticate signatures, prove creditor allegations or resolve enforceability. Calculations cover the collective named-maker obligation; they do not establish identity with the regulated Michael Edward Bleich, CRD 4262671. Identity finding; makers and apparent execution.

Financial reconstruction

Contract terms and notice limitations

Section 1 sets 10% per annum, compounded annually, actual/360 on outstanding principal. Section 7 supplies 15%, likewise annually compounded and actual/360, while an Event of Default continues after cure. Payment defaults allow ten Business Days; other covenant defaults have ten Business Days after written notice. Acceleration separately requires delivered written election. These are readings of the clauses, without an enforceability determination. Terms finding; note, page 1, page 2.

Section 8 prescribes written notices at designated addresses by hand, recognized overnight service or certified mail with return receipt requested. Broad waivers in sections 5/16 coexist with express notice/election terms; section 16 excepts section 10 assignment notice. The demand’s “Via Federal Express” legend and Sosnick’s delivery assertion lack independent receipt/tracking. Payment cure, other-covenant notice, acceleration election and default interest remain distinct provisions; their interaction is unresolved. Notice finding; note, page 3, page 4, demand.
