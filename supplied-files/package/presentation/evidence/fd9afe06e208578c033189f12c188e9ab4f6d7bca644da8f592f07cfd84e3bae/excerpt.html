SUPREME COURT OF THE STATE OF NEW YORK

COUNTY OF NEW YORK

~---X
OPEH LLC,

Plaintiff, Index No.
-against- NOTICE OF MOTION
FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT
MICHAEL E. BLEICH and IN LIEU OF COMPLAINT
ALEXIS K. BLEICH,
Defendants.
~--X

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that upon the annexed affidavit of Aaron Sosnick, sworn to
on September 26, 2018, with exhibits annexed thereto, and the accompanying Memorandum of
Law, dated September 24, 2018, Plaintiff OPEH LLC will move this Court on November 20,
2018, 2018, at 9:30 A.M., or as soon thereafter as counsel may be heard, in Room 130, 60 Centre
Street, New York, New York, 10007, for an order, pursuant to CPLR §3213, granting Plaintiff
OPEH LLC summary judgment in lieu of complaint in the sum of $2,233,007, together with
interest accruing from September 17, 2018 until paid, at the agreed upon rate of 15% per annum
($930.42 per diem), and the accrued, unpaid interest incurred to date in the amount $155,070,
against Defendants Michael E. Bleich and Alexis K. Bleich, based upon a promissory note dated
January 11, 2018; executed by Defendants Michael E. Bleich and Alexis K. Bleich, an
instrument for the payment of money only, a true copy of which is submitted herewith; and
granting Plaintiff OPEH LLC reasonable costs of collection including, without limitation,
reasonable attorneys’ fees, and the costs and disbursements of this action, together with such

other and further relief as the Court deems just and proper.
