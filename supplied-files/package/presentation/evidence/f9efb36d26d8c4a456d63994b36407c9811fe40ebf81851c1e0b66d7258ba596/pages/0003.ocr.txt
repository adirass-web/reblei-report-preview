6. Amendments. No amendment to any provision of this Note shall in any event be effective
unless the same shall be in writing and signed by Holder and Makers.

7. Default. The occurrence of any of the following shall constitute an “Event of Default” under
this Note:

Gi) the Makers shall fail to pay within ten (10) Business Days any interest
when due;

(ii) the Makers shall fail to pay within ten (10) Business Days the Principal
Amount when due;

Git) the Makers shall fail to perform or observe any other term or condition
binding hereunder and such failure shall continue unremedied for a period of ten (10)
Business Days after written notice thereof from any Holder,

(iv) the Makers file a voluntary petition under any bankruptcy, reorganization,
or insolvency law of any jurisdiction;

(v) the Makers consent to or apply for appointment of a trustee, receiver,
custodian, or similar official for itself or for all or substantially all of its assets;

(vl) a trustee, recetver, custodian, or similar official is appointed to take
possession of all or substantially all of the Makers’ assets and such appointment is not

dismissed within thirty (30) days after appomtment;

(vii) the Makers make any assignment for the benefit of creditors, or other
arrangement or composition under any laws for the benefit of insolvents which is not

dismissed within thirty (30) days;

(viii) an order for relief is entered against the Makers under any bankruptcy,
reorganization, or insolvency law of any jurisdiction and is not dismissed or stayed within
thirty (30) days after its entry; or any case, proceeding, or other action seeking such order
remains undismissed for thirty (30) days after its filing; or

Gx) any lien, writ of attachment, garnishment, or execution is levied against all
or substantially all of the Makers’ assets, including but in no way limited to the Property;
or all or substantially all of the Makers’ assets, including but in no way limited to the
Property, become subject to any lien, attachment, garnishment, execution, or other
judicial seizure, and the same is not satisfied, removed, released, or bonded within thirty
(30) days after the date the lien was filed, the writ was levied or the date of the
attachment, garnishment, execution, or other judicial seizure.

If an Event of Default shall occur and be continuing, the entire unpaid Principal Amount
of this Note and accrued interest thereon shall immediately become due and payable upon the
delivery of written notice to the Makers that the Holder elects to accelerate payment of this Note.

Failure to exercise such option shall not constitute a waiver of any of the Holder’s rights to
exercise such option in the event of a subsequent default.
