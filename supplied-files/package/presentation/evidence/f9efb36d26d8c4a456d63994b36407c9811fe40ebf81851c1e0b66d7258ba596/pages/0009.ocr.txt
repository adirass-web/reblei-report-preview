Additionally, the Promissory Note provides that, in the event of a default, a default rate of
interest (“Default Rate’’) will be applied to during any period an Event of Default that has
occurred and to the amount of principal then outstanding at a rate equal to 15% per annum,
compounded annually. See, Sosnick Aff. at ¥13 and, Exhibit A of the Sosnick Aff.

Pursuant to the Promissory Note, the total amount due to OPEH on September 17, 2018
was: (i) $155,070 in accrued and unpaid interest; and, (ii) the Principal Amount of $2,233,007, or
$2,388,077.

Defendants failed to make any payments pursuant to the Promissory Note. See, Sosnick
Aff. at 998 and 9.

Defendants’ initial event of default occurred when Defendant failed to pay OPEH interest
in the amount of $155,070, pursuant to the Promissory Note, on the Maturity Date. See, Sosnick
Aff. at ¢6 and 8; and, Exhibit A of the Sosnick Aff.

Defendants’ other event of default occurred when Defendant failed to pay OPEH the
Principal Amount of $2,233,007 pursuant to the Promissory Note on the Maturity Date. See,
Sosnick Aff. at §§6 and 9; and, Exhibit A of the Sosnick Aff.

The Default Rate applies to the total balance due of or $2,233,007 is 15% per annum
($930.42 per diem).

The Promissory Note also provides that Defendants will pay the costs of collection and
reasonable attorneys’ fees in the event of default. See, Sosnick Aff. at 414 and Exhibit A of the
Sosnick Aff.
