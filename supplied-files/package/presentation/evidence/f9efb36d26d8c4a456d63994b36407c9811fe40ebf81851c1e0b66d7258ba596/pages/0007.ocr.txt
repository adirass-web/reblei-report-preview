PRELIMINARY STATEMENT

Plaintiff, OPEH LLC (“Plaintiff or “OPEH”), by its attorneys, Tarter Krinsky & Drogin
LLP, submits this memorandum of law in support of its motion for summary judgment in lieu of
complaint seeking the sum $2,233,007, together with interest accruing from September 17, 2018
until paid, at the agreed upon rate of 15% per annum ($930.42 per diem), and the accrued unpaid
interest in the amount of $155,070, plus OPEH’s reasonable costs of collection including,
without limitation, reasonable attorneys’ fees, and the costs of this action.

On January 11, 2018, Defendants duly executed a promissory note (the “Promissory
Note”), an instrument for the payment of money only, promising to pay OPEH, inter alia,
$2,233,007, for value received from OPEH. Defendants failed to make any payments to OPEH
pursuant to the Promissory Note. Because Defendants failed to make any payments whatsoever
pursuant to the Promissory Note, the OPEH is entitled to summary judgment in the sum of
$2,233,007, together with interest accruing until paid at the agreed upon rate of 15% per annum
($930.42 per diem), and the accrued unpaid interest in the amount of $155,070, plus OPEH’s
reasonable costs of collection including, without limitation, reasonable attorneys’ fees, and the
costs of this action, in accordance with the terms of the Promissory Note.
