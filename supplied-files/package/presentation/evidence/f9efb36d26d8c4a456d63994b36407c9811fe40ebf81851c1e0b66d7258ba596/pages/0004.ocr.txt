9. Invalidity. Any provision hereof which is to any extent invalid or unenforceable shall be
ineffective only to the extent of such invalidity or unenforceability without invalidating the
remaining provisions hereof, and each provision of this Note shall be valid and enforceable to the
fullest extent permitted by law.

10. This Note is fully assignable and transferable by Holder in Holder’s discretion, and the
assignee or transferee party shall become “Holder” for all purposes of this Note upon (but not
prior to) written notice of such assignment or transfer being given to Makers. Makers shall not
be responsible to any subsequent Holder of this Note on account of any payment made by
Makers to a prior Holder prior to receipt of notice of assignment in the manner contemplated by
the preceding sentence.

11. This Note is and shall be binding upon Makers and Makers’ estates, heirs, successors and
assigns. No assignment or delegation of any of the obligations of Makers under this Note shall
release Makers therefrom, except as otherwise agreed in writing by the then Holder of this Note.

12. It being the intent of the Parties that the interest charged herein shall comply with applicable
usury laws. As such, in the event any interest is charged and/or paid on this Note that is deemed
to be in excess of the then legal maximum rate, then that portion of the interest payment
representing an amount in excess of the then legal maximum rate shall be deemed a payment of
principal and applied against the principal of this Note.
