1. Interest. Interest shall accrue on the principal balance from time to time outstanding on this
Note at a rate of 10% per annum, compounded annually. All interest shall be calculated on the
basis of a year of three hundred sixty (360) days for the actual number of days elapsed. All
accrued and unpaid interest shall be payable on the Maturity Date. If the day on which any
payment due under this Note is not a Business Day, then such amount due on such date will be
due on the immediately subsequent Business Day. The term “Business Day” when used herein
means a weekday, except a legal holiday or a day on which banking institutions in the State of
New York are authorized by law to be closed.

Financing Statement against all of Makers’ right, title and interest in a certain condominium

Property”), including but not limited to all appliances, fixtures and other property located at the
Property, as well as all of the assets and property of Makers, all of which are identified as
“Collateral” in the UCC-1, to secure repayment of this Note. At all times during which any
obligation under this Note is outstanding, Makers hereby grant to the Holder a security interest in
and lien on the Property and the “Collateral” and proceeds from the sale of the Property and
Collateral identified in the UCC-1. Makers shall make, execute and endorse, and acknowledge
and deliver or file, or cause the same to be done, all such additional agreements, undertakings,
conveyances, transfers, assignments, financing statements, and other assurances, and take any
and all such other action, as Holder may, from time to time, deem reasonably necessary or proper
in connection with this Note or the obligations under this Note, or for granting to the Holder any
security for the obligations under this Note. which the Holder may reasonably request from time
to time, including, without limitation, filing UCC-1 financing statements (and amendments
thereto) reflecting its respective security interest in the Collateral, and the Property and all
proceeds from the sale of the Property.

3. Repayment. Principal and interest are payable in the lawful currency of the United States of
America, in immediately available funds, without any defense, set-off, counterclaim, rescission,
recoupment or deduction of any kind. All payments shall be made to Holder by check, certified
funds delivered to the Holder at the above address or by wire transfer of immediately available
funds to an account identified in writing to the Makers by the Holder. Any payment by Makers
to Holder shall be applied first to payment of accrued, unpaid interest and then to payment of the
outstanding principal balance of this Note. Any remaining outstanding principal balance of this
Note, and any accrued but unpaid interest thereon, shall be due and payable either on the date
upon which the Makers close the sale of, or otherwise transfer the Property or on September 1,
2018, whichever occurs first (the “Maturity Date”).
