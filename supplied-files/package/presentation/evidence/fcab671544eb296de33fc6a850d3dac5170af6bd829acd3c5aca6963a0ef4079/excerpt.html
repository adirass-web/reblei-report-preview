affidavit in support of OPEH’s motion, submitted pursuant to CPLR §3213, for summary
judgment in lieu of complaint in the sum of $2,233,007, together with interest accruing until paid

to make any payments pursuant to a promissory note, an instrument for the payment of money
only, promising to pay OPEH, inter alia, $2,233,007, for value received.

2. Upon information and belief, defendants Michael E. Bleich and Alexis K. Bleich
(the “Defendants”) are individuals residing at 13 Harrison Street, Unit 1, New York, New York

3. In consideration for OPEH’s loan of $2,233,007 (the “Principal Amount”) to
Defendants, Defendants delivered a promissory note as security to OPEH in the amount of
$2,233,007 (the ‘Promissory Note”) on or about January 11, 2018. A true copy of the
Promissory Note is annexed hereto as Exhibit A.

4. The Promissory Note states that the principal balance and interest accrued thereon
are due and payable by Defendants to OPEH, on September |, 2018 (the “Maturity Date”). See,
Section 3, Exhibit A.

5. The Promissory Note states that “[i]nterest shall accrue on the principal balance
from time to time outstanding on this Note at a rate of 10% per annum, compounded annually.
All interest shall be calculated on the basis of a year of three hundred sixty (360) days for the
actual number of days elapsed. All accrued and unpaid interest shall be payable on the Maturity
Date.” See, Section 1, Exhibit A.

6. The Promissory Note defines an Event of Default, among other things, as “[t]he
occurrence of any of the following shall constitute an "Event of Default" under this Note: (i) the
[Defendant]s shall fail to pay within ten (10) Business Days any interest when due; (ii) the
[Defendant]s shall fail to pay within ten (10) Business Days the Principal Amount when due ...”
See, Section 7, Exhibit A.

7. The Promissory Note states that “[i]f an Event of Default shall occur and be
continuing, the entire unpaid Principal Amount of this Note and accrued interest thereon shall
immediately become due and payable upon the delivery of written notice to the [Defendant]s that
[OPEH] elects to accelerate payment of this Note. Failure to exercise such option shall not
constitute a waiver of any of the [OPEH]'s rights to exercise such option in the event of a

subsequent default.” See, Section 7, Exhibit A.
