Section 1 sets **10% per annum, compounded annually, actual/360** on outstanding principal. Section 7 supplies **15%**, likewise annually compounded and actual/360, while an Event of Default continues after cure. Payment defaults allow ten Business Days; other covenant defaults have ten Business Days after written notice. Acceleration separately requires delivered written election. These are readings of the clauses, without an enforceability determination.
