Assumptions and limits

The income statement uses “Net Income” for both an earlier subtotal and the final after-tax result. The $5,469,261 used here is the final after-tax result, also carried into the cash-flow statement.

The $5,589,439 subtotal appears in the financing section, but the original labels it “Net Cash Provided By Operating Activities.” The original wording is preserved here as a source inconsistency.

Members’ distributions and advances are firm-level reported amounts. Ownership bands, control roles and signatures do not allocate a receipt to Michael Bleich or any other member. The report is not bank verification.

Negative operating cash does not by itself establish misconduct. This sample compares one year; missing later flow statements are not treated as zero.
