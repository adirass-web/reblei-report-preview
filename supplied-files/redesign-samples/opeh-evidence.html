Assumptions and limits

The calculations use one collective principal, not a sum of separate maker debts. The note’s predecessor recital concerns Ophir Partners LLC and a personal guaranty; predecessor instruments, amounts and any new cash disbursement are not independently established.

January 11 is the assumed accrual start. Unchanged principal and no payments or fees are assumed; the supplied material lacks a transaction ledger. Annual capitalization is assumed at the note-date anniversary, which is not reached in this interval. Partial-year interest is linear actual/360, without daily compounding.

The 249-day branch includes one endpoint and excludes the other. The 250-day branch counts both endpoint date labels. The note supplies no endpoint-inclusion or interim-rounding rule.

The $620.35 and $0.07 differences subtract the unrounded accepted calculations from the $155,070 assertion, then round the differences to cents. Accepted exact decimals are stored at Decimal precision 50; repeating-decimal strings retain that accepted computational precision.

The whole-dollar branch retains the same exact 250-day interest and changes the final display quantum. The daily-cent branch rounds $620.279722… to $620.28 before multiplying by 250. Both are conditional explanations for numerical agreement, not evidence of the claimant’s actual worksheet.

If the asserted base already includes September 17, separately requested additional interest “from” that date requires a nonoverlapping boundary explanation. The supplied claimant methodology does not resolve that question.

The scenarios establish neither default, enforceability, overcharge, payment status, a current balance nor a money award. The litigation maker’s identity is not independently linked here to the regulated Michael Edward Bleich.
