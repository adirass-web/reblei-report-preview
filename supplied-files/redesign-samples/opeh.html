Two rounding choices can reproduce the assertion

Round once at the end: 250-day result $155,069.930555… → $155,070 at whole dollars.

Round the daily rate first: $620.28 × 250 → $155,070.00.

Daily-cent rounding over 249 days yields $154,449.72 and does not alone explain the assertion. A numerical match does not identify the creditor’s method. These conditional alternatives establish neither an overcharge nor an amount due.

If the asserted base already includes September 17, additional interest requested “from” that date requires a nonoverlapping boundary explanation.
