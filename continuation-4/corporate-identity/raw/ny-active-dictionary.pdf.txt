Dataset Overview Document 
NYSDOS Active Corporations and Other Business Entities 
 
Data Collection: Data was collected by running a SQL query against a database that is used for the Department 
of State’s business entity public search function (http://www.dos.ny.gov/corps/bus_entity_search.html).  
Only DOS process / service of process address is required for incorporation.  Registered agent is optional.  DOS 
process / service of process addresses may be an individual or corporation designated to handle legal 
proceedings for the designated corporation and may not have any bearing on the physical address of the 
corporation or any of its officers. 
Names and addresses for the Chairman or CEO and location fields are not collected until the entity’s first 
“biennial statement” which is two year following their initial registration.
