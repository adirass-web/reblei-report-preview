NYS Department of State 
Corporations and Other Entities: All Filings 
Overview 
 
General Description:  
 
The New York State Department of State, one of the oldest and most diverse agencies in state 
government, was established in 1778, and is known as the Keeper of Records.  
The Department’s Division of Corporations, State Records and Uniform Commercial Code is the 
filing office for documents relating to domestic and foreign business and not-for-profit 
corporations, limited liability companies, limited partnerships, registered limited liability 
partnerships and other entities that are formed or authorized to conduct business or activities 
under the laws of New York State. 
This dataset contains information related to Domestic & Foreign Entity Filings maintained in a 
centralized electronic database that is used to index corporation and business entity records 
filed in the Department of State’s Division of Corporations, State Records and Uniform 
Commercial Code. 
 
Data Collection Methodology:  
 
Domestic Business corporations, not-for-profit corporations, limited liability companies and 
limited partnerships must file a formation document with the Department’s Division of 
Corporations, State Records and Uniform Commercial Code.  Out-of-state or “foreign” business 
corporations, not-for-profit corporations, limited liability companies and limited partnerships 
which choose to do business or conduct activities in New York State must file with the 
Department of State an Application for Authority.  If the foreign entity’s name is not 
distinguishable from the names of all other entities on file with the Division or acceptable under 
the applicable law, the entity must use a “fictitious” name.    
 
This dataset also includes registrations of Registered Limited Liability Partnerships and New 
York Registered Foreign Limited Liability Partnerships.  It also includes corporate or other 
business entity “Assumed Names” filed pursuant to General Business Law §130. 
This dataset does not include names of corporations or other entities, which are formed or 
authorized by other filing offices, such as: Banking Corporations, Insurance Corporations, 
Education Corporations, and most Religious Corporations. Additionally, this data set does not 
include the names of general partnerships and sole proprietorships. 
 
Statistical and Analytic Issues:  
 
Data is extracted once a week from a centralized electronic database that is used to index 
corporation and business entity records filed in the Department of State’s Division of

Corporations, State Records and Uniform Commercial Code. Active, inactive, and suspended 
entity name data is extracted and made available.  
 
The term “active” indicates, that according to the records of the Department’s Division of 
Corporations, a domestic corporation, limited liability company, or limited partnership exists 
and that the entity has not dissolved. In the case of a Registered Limited Liability Partnership 
the term “active” means the entity is currently registered as a registered as a registered limited 
liability partnership and such registration has not been withdrawn or revoked.  In the case of a 
foreign limited partnership or foreign limited liability company the term “active” means the 
entity has filed an application for authority and is authorized to do business in New York State 
and such authority to do business has not been surrendered, terminated, or annulled. In the 
case or a New York Registered Foreign Limited Liability Partnership, the term “active” means 
the entity has filed a notice to do business as a New York Registered Foreign Limited Liability 
Company and such notice has not been withdrawn or revoked or the authority of such entity 
suspended.  
 
 
The term “inactive” indicates, that according to the records of the Department’s Division of 
Corporations, State Records, and Uniform Commercial Code, a domestic corporation, limited 
liability company, or limited partnership no longer exists. In the case of a Registered Limited 
Liability Partnership the term “inactive” means the entity is no longer registered as a registered 
as a registered limited liability partnership.  In the case of a foreign corporation, limited liability 
company, limited partnership the term “inactive” means the entity has is no longer authorized 
to do business or conduct activities in New York State. In the case or a New York Registered 
Foreign Limited Liability Partnership, the term “inactive” means the entity has withdrawn its 
notice to do business as a New York Registered Foreign Limited Liability Company or such 
notice has been revoked.  
 
Existing domestic and foreign entities that have had their authority to conduct business or 
activities suspended will have a “Suspended” entity status. 
 
 
Limitations of Data Use:  
 
Data in this dataset is intended for general public information only.  Data is not to be construed 
as an official record of the Department of State, nor is the presence of the entity in this data to 
be construed as the current legal status of the entity.  The data included in this dataset may not 
be construed as having the legal effect of a certified copy or certification of any of any such 
data. Addresses included in the dataset may or may not reflect the actual physical location of 
the entity itself or any of its officers.  Data is only accurate as of the date indicated. For the 
most current information and legally acceptable documentation, contact the New York State 
Department of State.  The New York State Department of State recognizes the possibility of 
human and/or mechanical error in producing this dataset.

Related Datasets 
 
This dataset, Corporations and Other Entities: All Filings, which contains information related to 
Domestic & Foreign Entity Filings, along with other Data files listed below will provide all filing 
information maintained in a centralized electronic database that is used to index corporation 
and business entity records filed in the Department of State’s Division of Corporations, State 
Records and Uniform Commercial Code.  Files that are available and can be linked include:    
- 
All Filings - This file contains all a list of all documents filed with the Division that are 
stored in the electronic database. 
- 
All Filings - Name Status:  This file contains current and previous names for each entity. 
- 
All Filings - Stock:  This file contains share information for each entity that has shares.  
- 
All Filings - Status History:  This file contains status information for each entity.    
- 
All Filings - Constituent:  This file contains information on merger or consolidation 
constituents. 
- 
All Filings - Limited Partnerships:  This file contains the names of domestic and foreign 
Limited Partnerships that file Certificates of Adoption.   
- 
All Filings - Address:  This file contains all the address information for entities.
