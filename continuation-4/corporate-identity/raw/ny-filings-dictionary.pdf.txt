Data Description 
DOS ID Number 
Text 
DOS Unique ID number for each entity 
Filing Num 
Text 
Unique ID Number for each document filed 
Date Filed 
Text 
The date the document was filed by Department of State 
Approved Date 
Text 
Date the document was approved by the Department of 
State for filing 
Eff Date 
Text 
Effective Date of the document filed  
Duration Date 
Text 
The date on which a corporation is dissolved by 
expiration of its period of duration or the date after 
which the Limited Liability Company or Limited 
Partnership is to dissolve 
Dis Eff Date 
Text 
Date on which a dissolution becomes effective 
Foreign Formation Date 
Text 
Date on which a foreign entity was formed in the 
jurisdiction of its formation 
Mod Cert Code 
Text 
The mod cert code, taken together with the information 
in the LAW field, is a description of the type of document 
represented by this record

Text 
Type of entity (e.g., “business corporation”) 
Document Type 
Text 
Type of document filed (e.g., “certificate of 
incorporation”)

The law under which the document was filed

Text 
Category (“Charitable” or “Non-Charitable”) of the Not-
for-Profit Corporation

 True/Real name of an entity

Amd Fict Name Flag 
Text 
Fictitious name indicator for the filing.  '1' means 
maintain the fictitious name, '2' means the fictitious 
name was amended or changed, '3' means the fictitious 
name is being dropped or deactivated, '4' means a 
fictitious name was added to the entity 
Amd Foreign formation Date 
Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change to the formation date of a foreign 
entity.  This is the date when a foreign entity was formed 
in the jurisdiction of its formation   
Amd For Juris Flag 
Text 
‘X' indicates a change to the foreign entity’s jurisdiction 
of formation 
Amd NFP Category Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change to the Not-For-Profit Corporation 
category 
Amd SOP Address Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change to the Service of Process address 
Amd Prov. Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change to a provision in the document 
indicated. This is a catch-all for amendments or changes 
not covered by other amendment flags 
Amd Purps. Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates an amendment to the Purpose of an entity 
Amd Reg Agt Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change in the Registered Agent’s address 
Amd Share Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates a change in the Share information 
Amd Loc. Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates change to the Business Corporation’s 
principal executive office 
Amd Admit Prtnr Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates the addition of a general partner to a 
limited partnership 
Amd Withdr Prtnr Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates the withdraw of a general partner from a 
limited partnership 
Restate Cert Flag 
Text 
'X' indicates the filing is a Restated Certificate of 
Incorporation 
Amd Chair Flag 
 
'X' indicates the addition or amendment to the Name 
and/or business address of the Chief Executive Office  
Card File Flag 
Text 
Indicates the Corporations and Other Business Entity 
Database does not include the entire filing record history 
for the entity.  'F' means the document is the first record 
in the filing history and that other prior document(s) are 
not on file with DOS.  'D' means that only part of the 
filing history is in the Database and that the DOS Card 
Index includes information on other documents filed 
with DOS 
Constituent Flag 
Text 
A 'C' indicates that the entity is a constituent of a merger 
or consolidation and that additional information for the 
constituent is not in the Database, is not available, or is 
maintained in the DOS Card File
