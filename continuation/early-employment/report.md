# Early employment: original records and remaining gaps

Research date: November 4, 2026. Only newly created investigation outputs and independently retrieved sources were used. Legacy repository material was not read.

**An original document retrieved from Lehman's court-appointed claims agent lists “Bleich, Michael Edward” as a restricted stock unit agreement counterparty.** This corroborates the reported Lehman connection. It supplies no CRD, role title or employment dates, so the match to the subject remains provisional.

## Source chain

1. A May 20, 2010 opinion from the Southern District of New York Bankruptcy Court identifies Epiq Bankruptcy Solutions LLC as the Debtors' claims agent (physical PDF page 5).
2. Epiq's public docket response identifies docket 3078, LBHI case 08-13555, schedules filed March 12, 2009, and Part 2 document 1095348.
3. The original Part 2 PDF has 476 pages. The name appears in Schedule G, Restricted Stock Unit Agreements, at **physical page 52**, printed **RSU page 34 of 413**, **LBHI Schedules 349**.
4. A separately preserved CourtListener mirror has the corresponding table and an ECF stamp for docket 3078-1, filed March 12, 2009. Its bytes differ from the Epiq original. Both copies remain separately identified.

[Original Epiq attachment](https://document.epiq11.com/document/getdocumentbycode?docId=1095348&projectCode=LBH&source=DM). SHA-256: `61e73a9906da1f4f941dea08a4e2403dc9d758964349ee618bf4917091be3a18`.

This is a **debtor-filed schedule statement**, not a court finding that an award was valid, vested or payable. It establishes neither an employment interval nor an award date or value. LBHI is the debtor; a named contract counterparty is not thereby a bankruptcy debtor. No misconduct inference follows.

## Independent claims-index check

Epiq's exact-phrase search for Michael Edward Bleich returned zero results. Searching the surname Bleich returned one scheduled record: **BLEICH, MICHAEL EDWARD**, schedule **13555000004788**, with `claimNumber: null` and `imageDocumentId: null`.

That record is not described here as a filed or allowed claim. Selected professional fields were retained; address and other personal fields were excluded. The different search outcomes show why a single exact-phrase negative does not establish absence.

## Access limits and preservation

Three exact-name requests to the SEC EDGAR full-text service returned HTTP 403. These are access failures, not completed negative searches. The earlier FINRA/IAPD individual-report gaps remain. Public Epiq retrieval succeeded without an account or payment. No outreach, new account or paid records were used.

The next gap is an original archived individual registration history or employer/court document with exact employment dates. This pass recovered useful original professional records but does not close that gap.
