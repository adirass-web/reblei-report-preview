Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. Chapter 11 Proceedings Examiner’s Report

Jenner & Block is providing links to the Report of the Examiner in the Chapter 11 proceedings of Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. The Examiner’s report, reproduced below in individual Adobe Acrobat PDF files, is divided into nine volumes and features hyperlinks to all of the documents cited in the report’s over 8,000 footnotes.

The Examiner in this matter was Anton R. Valukas, Chairman of Jenner & Block. Please direct any inquires regarding the below report to: lehmaninquiry@jenner.com.

Examiner's Report:

  • Volume 1- Sections I & II: Introduction, Executive Summary & Procedural Background; Section III.A.1: Risk
  • Volume 2- Section III.A.2: Valuation; Section III.A.3: Survival
  • Volume 3- Section III.A.4: Repo 105
  • Volume 4- Section III.A.5: Secured Lenders; Section III.A.6: Government
  • Volume 5- Section III.B: Avoidance Actions; Section III.C: Barclays Transaction
  • Volume 6- Appendix 1
  • Volume 7- Appendices 2 - 7
  • Volume 8- Appendices 8 - 22
  • Volume 9- Appendices 23 - 34
