# Dossier editorial standard

Adopted November 4, 2026 for the in-house diligence report and evidence dossier. This standard applies to authored narrative. It does not change source documents, quotations, claim records or evidence classifications.

## Skills and suitability

Use [Plain English](https://github.com/b1rdmania/claude-plain-english-skill/blob/main/SKILL.md) for the narrative drafting and editing pass, with its [reference](https://github.com/b1rdmania/claude-plain-english-skill/blob/main/REFERENCE.md). It suits concise explanations and removes unnecessary wording. The skill itself excludes direct quotations and meaning-critical legal terminology.

Use the available Humanizer skill as a final prose review: remove generic framing, repetition and inflated claims while preserving meaning. Keep the tone neutral. Do not add personality, opinions, drama or an overall risk verdict.

Plain English is adopted as task guidance, not installed as a global plugin. Its companion Simple English is intended for technical procedures; do not apply both rule sets to the same passage. Neither a vocabulary substitution list nor a readability target overrides factual precision.

## Report structure

Write a standalone report about the findings, not a diary of research rounds.

1. **Scope and principal findings.** Identify the subject and research cut-off. Explain what the records establish and the main unresolved questions.
2. **Career chronology.** Separate dated role disclosures from verified employment intervals. Put unverified early-career leads in a clearly marked subsection.
3. **Entities and ownership.** Explain the Scout/Ophir and EntryPoint structures separately, with dated snapshots and the relevant identifiers. Keep Inferent conditional.
4. **Investment-management events.** Identify the firm or person involved and the record's procedural status. Separate administrative filings, regulatory matters and arbitration or litigation.
5. **Financial observations.** Define each metric and its date basis before comparing values. Keep incompatible measures separate.
6. **Unresolved questions and next actions.** Link the separate missing-record instructions. State the evidence needed to resolve each question.
7. **Evidence dossier.** Provide the claim and document registers, source locators, conflicts and coverage records behind the narrative.

Keep the executive account short enough to orient a reviewer. Put detailed filing sequences and identifiers in tables or linked records when they interrupt the explanation. There is no arbitrary word limit that permits dropping a material finding.

## How to write each finding

State the finding first. Name the filing or authority that supports it. Place the material limitation beside the claim. Provide a stable claim link and the exact source locator.

Use short paragraphs, active verbs when the actor is known, and familiar words. Explain an acronym at first use. Use one consistent name for each entity or financial measure. Distinguish exact legal names and unresolved historical names rather than smoothing them into a single label.

Use tables for comparisons and dated sequences; use prose to explain their meaning. Use sentence-case headings and selective emphasis. Remove repeated introductions, generic conclusions, promotional language and research-process narration.

## Qualifications that must survive editing

- Preserve the difference between a firm's filed statement and an adjudicated finding.
- Preserve allegation, settlement, award, administrative withdrawal and payment status.
- Keep firm outcomes separate from personal sanctions. A signature for a firm does not make the signer the respondent.
- Keep official-record inference visibly labeled. Remove redundant hedges only when the remaining sentence has exactly the same evidentiary strength.
- State the scope of a negative search. A missing result does not establish that a role, entity or proceeding never existed.
- Preserve unknown dates and distinguish filing, signature, registration, acquired-status, measurement and cessation dates.
- Keep ownership bands, financial units and discrepancies intact. Do not convert ranges into exact stakes.
- Retain NYSCEF as an access gap and the prior Library files as unreconciled.
- Preserve identifiers, quotations, URLs and page/field locators exactly.

Do not mechanically replace words such as approximately, termination, control or material where they carry a specific meaning. Never replace suggests or supports with proves for stylistic force. Necessary qualifications are evidence, not verbal clutter.

## Editorial workflow and acceptance

First organize the narrative around the reader's questions. Then apply Plain English to the draft, flagging wording before deciding whether to change it. Re-read the result for meaning and apply Humanizer's neutral factual-prose guidance to any remaining repetition or stock language.

Compare every revised passage with its source claim. Check names, numbers, dates, qualifiers and citations. Keep a brief edit record when a change could affect interpretation. This is an editorial fidelity check, not new factual research.

For the finished dossier, use the separately required independent evidence reviewer to check consequential passages after editing. Confirm that the printed report retains the qualifications and source references visible on screen.
