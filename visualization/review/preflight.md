# Visualization semantic preflight

Basis: pinned inputs/full-dossier.md, inputs/claims.md, inputs/evidence-index.json and source-snapshot.json (source commit 0d9bdb80d7c4e21a38e798eac58ea71e375ee5fb; research cut-off 2026-11-04). This is a representation review, with no new research.

- **Date basis must travel with every assertion.** Acquired-status month is not a transaction or appointment date; filing/signature dates are distinct. Scout firm registration and cessation do not establish Bleich employment endpoints (ID-C010). Ophir CEO April 2012 does not independently date CCO duties; March 25, 2015 is the first retrieved combined-title disclosure (EMP5-C03, EMP4-C04–C06). EntryPoint CIO May 2023 and interests July 2023 are acquired-status fields, not a complete employment interval (SEC-C002–C004, EMP5-C04).
- **Ownership arrows need owner, target, band and snapshot.** C=25%–<50%; D=50%–<75%; E=75%+; Schedule A NA=<5%. Do not multiply/add bands into exact downstream stakes or imply control is a numerical stake (EMP4-C07). Trustee Code F is not numerical beneficial ownership (EP5-C007, SEC-C015).
- **GP target trap.** SEC-C006 records EntryPoint GP LLC as GENERAL PARTNER on EntryPoint Capital’s Schedule A; do not silently redirect that row to EntryPoint Partners LP as if directly proved by that claim. A clearly labelled as-filed relationship is safest. EntryPoint GP LLC and EP Fund GP LLC are distinct; the latter is fund GP (SEC-C006, SEC-C012, feeder Form D role claims).
- **Separate historical snapshots.** Scout MEB→Finch 50%–<75% and NAYS→Finch 25%–<50% differ from Ophir March 2017 MEB→Finch 75%+. March 18 versus June 17, 2016 establishes disclosure change, not a dated sale or NAYS economic exit (ID-C006–C007, ID-C014, EMP4-C08). Finch ownership timing: June 1, 2012 affiliate narrative, August acquired-status and August 31 audited transition remain distinct (ID-C009, CORP4-C03–C06). MEB INC/MEB NY INC must retain unresolved labels (EMP4-C09).
- **Current versus historical EntryPoint.** March 2023–June 2024 United Master chain is historical (EP5-C007). October 2026 trust Code C GP member is a different snapshot (SEC-C015). Do not combine into one current chain, identify UNITED INTERNATIONAL BANK CORP with the Curaçao N.V. candidate, or promote candidate registry joins to verified identities (CORP5-C008).
- **Firm sanctions are not Bleich sanctions.** Nasdaq AWC accepted April 7, 2015: Scout respondent; censure and $3 million, without admitting/denying, without adjudication (EVT-C001–C004). Three Cboe orders are Scout-only settlements, not extra personal sanctions or duplicates of FINRA disclosure events (LIT-C001–C009, EVT-C006–C008). Wedbush arbitration payment/collection is unestablished; do not conflate award, fine and payment.
- **Conditional Inferent branch.** Firm existence and records do not establish Bleich role, ownership or Ophir succession (EMP5-C05–C07, INF3-C006). Shared named executives are not a Bleich identity bridge (EP5-C008–C009). Avoid a solid career-path arrow from Ophir through Inferent to EntryPoint.
- **Lehman remains provisional.** Exact full-name RSU-counterparty entry filed March 12, 2009 lacks CRD; no proved role, employment interval, award value/vesting or personal bankruptcy (EARLY-C003). September 19, 2006 corporate quote is a lead, not CRD-confirmed appointment (EARLY-C005). Chase/Barclays dates unverified.
- **Administrative registration is not closure or discipline.** Ophir advisory cessation June 23, 2017; withdrawal filed/termination June 29, not personal departure/dissolution (EP5-C005, ID-C020). Inferent March 21, 2020 withdrawal expressly says business not ceased and exemption continuation (EP5-C006). EntryPoint December 10, 2021 withdrawal reports business not ceased and eligibility reason (EP5-C004); June 27, 2024 initial-registration/final-ERA flags establish reporting transition, not approval or compliance conclusions (EP5-C003).
- **Maps must identify exact claim and source IDs with locators.** Earlier claim wording can be qualified by later claims/dossier prose. “Verified as filed” supports attributed disclosure, not independently adjudicated fact. An omission is bounded to that disclosure/search and never universal negative proof.
