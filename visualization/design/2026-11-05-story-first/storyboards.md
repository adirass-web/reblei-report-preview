# Three storyboards — composition before code

These are low-fidelity editorial specifications, not finished charts. Claims refer to the [pinned claim appendix](../../inputs/claims.md); the [source snapshot](../../source-snapshot.json) identifies the reviewed inputs. Each concept has one reader question, a supported headline, a composition and a misconception check.

## 1. A small direct stake and reported control are different facts

**Reader question:** Does Bleich's below-5% direct interest describe his whole disclosed relationship to EntryPoint Capital?

**Working headline:** “EntryPoint reports a below-5% direct interest for Bleich, alongside control and a CIO role.”

**Subtitle:** EntryPoint Capital LLC, CRD 313946 — October 15, 2026 Form ADV; acquired-status month May 2023. Bleich CRD 4262671.

**Composition:** Two clearly labelled entities, Bleich at left and EntryPoint Capital at right. Three aligned relationship rows connect the same pair: “Direct ownership: less than 5%,” “Control: yes, as filed,” and “Professional role: chief investment officer.” Ownership receives the accent; control and role use separate directly labelled rows. The graphic's organizing principle is three different meanings attached to the same person–firm pair. No proportional slice and no monetary value.

**Reading sequence:** Headline → the below-5% ownership row → the separate control and role rows → local scope note.

**Beside the graphic:** “Direct interest only. Separate indirect interests are disclosed and are the subject of the next chart. The acquired-status field is not an independently verified appointment date.”

**Evidence:** SEC-C002. Context for the following chart: SEC-C003, SEC-C004, SEC-C005 and PAR-B1031410-OWN. All are filed disclosures, not an independent assessment of actual operational control.

**Follow-on story, not another branch in this chart:** Bleich's interest in EntryPoint Partners and its interest in Capital; the separate GP interest is kept distinct. No inferred GP-to-Partners arrow.

**Text equivalent:** The October 2026 filing reports Bleich's direct interest in EntryPoint Capital below 5%, reports control, and lists him as CIO. These fields describe different relationships and do not determine his exact total economic stake.

**Reader test:** Identify which field measures ownership and which fields describe control or role. Reject “under 5% means he has no control” and “the chart calculates his total stake.”

## 2. The award assigns obligations to two recipients

**Reader question:** Under the Scout/Wedbush award, who was ordered to pay whom?

**Working headline:** “The award orders Wedbush to pay Scout and separately assesses hearing fees to FINRA.”

**Subtitle:** FINRA arbitration 15-02816 — served/indexed November 18, 2016; signature counterparts November 16.

**Composition:** Wedbush at left; two recipient rows at right. Top row: Scout Trading LLC, with two distinct amount labels, “$237,594 damages” and “$1,250 filing-fee reimbursement.” Bottom row: FINRA, with “$9,550 hearing-session assessment.” All connectors say “ordered/assessed obligation,” use equal thickness and have no flow animation or proportional ribbon. The focal distinction is the recipient, not a total recovery number.

**Next to the Scout row:** “Interest also specified: 4.5% annually from Aug 19, 2014 through the award date; then 9% until payment. No total calculated.” Preserve the source's unresolved legal boundary between signature/service/index dates; do not decide the transition date visually.

**Prominent scope strip:** “Payment not established. Scout's join to CRD 151990 is an official-record inference; the award supplies no CRD and does not name Bleich.” The Scout node itself uses the filed name, without silently attaching a confirmed CRD.

**Exclude:** Nasdaq fines, pleaded alternative damages requests, any Bleich node, speculative receipt of cash, and a combined principal-plus-interest recovery total. The regulatory respondent comparison is a separate story.

**Evidence:** LIT3-C04, LIT3-C05, LIT3-C07, LIT3-C09, LIT3-C10, LIT4-C01 and LIT5-C05.

**Text equivalent:** The award orders damages and filing-fee reimbursement to Scout, with interest terms, and assesses separate hearing fees to FINRA. These obligations do not show collection or establish Bleich as a party.

**Reader test:** Name the recipient of each amount. Reject “all three amounts are Scout's recovery,” “the award proves cash was paid,” and “the chart depicts Bleich's personal recovery.”

## 3. Three dated RAUM observations increase

**Reader question:** What do the comparable disclosed discretionary-RAUM observations show over time?

**Working headline:** “EntryPoint reports higher discretionary RAUM at three successive measurement dates.”

**Subtitle:** EntryPoint Capital LLC — US dollars, reported assets under management; measurement dates shown.

**Composition:** Three unconnected dots on a genuine calendar x-axis and a common y-axis in USD millions starting at zero. Label each dot directly:

- December 31, 2024: approximately $578.0m; exact disclosed figure approximately $578,038,743.
- March 11, 2026: approximately $700.3m; exact disclosed figure approximately $700,332,093.
- September 30, 2026: $999.2m; exact disclosed figure $999,157,153.

All three dots use one entity colour; the most recent dot has a slightly stronger outline and its date/value are emphasized. Do not draw an interpolated curve, smooth line or arrow suggesting a known path between observations. The longer first interval is visibly longer than the second.

**Directly below the plotting area:** “Change in reported RAUM is not investment return.” The source strip identifies the first two as brochure statements and the last as the reviewed ADV. Filing dates belong in source notes, not on this measurement-date axis.

**Exclude:** ERA Item 2B assets, master-fund gross assets, offering amounts sold, 13F holdings, inferred net inflows, fee revenue and personal wealth. These need different stories and definitions.

**Evidence:** PAR-B956158-RAUM, PAR-B1031410-RAUM, SEC-C007 and SEC-C008.

**Text equivalent:** The three reported discretionary-RAUM values increase across their stated measurement dates. The chart does not establish why they increased or calculate an investment return.

**Reader test:** Identify the entity, metric and measurement dates. Reject “72.9% investment return” or “the difference is net subscriptions.” No derived growth percentage is required for this story.

## Review before production

Review the question, headline, reading sequence, amount/date meaning and visible qualification for each storyboard. The three examples test relationship comparison, legal obligations and quantitative change. They do not commit the project to one renderer or visual style. Once the story is accepted, produce a design proof at normal report size before scaling to the rest of the catalogue.
