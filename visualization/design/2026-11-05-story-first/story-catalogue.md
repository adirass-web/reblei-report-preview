## Selection and sequence

Begin editorial review with S01, S03, S07, S08, S11 and S12. The detailed storyboards use S01, S08 and S11 to test three different relationship forms before production. The broader catalogue can expand when a reader question merits it; the first batch is not an exhaustive account.

Within the report, group stories by reader question: who controls or acts; who incurred an obligation; what the money measures mean; and what the record still cannot establish. Avoid implying a causal career narrative merely by placing unrelated charts next to each other.
