### Scope and reading rules

The investigation covers historical professional roles, advisers, broker-dealers, funds, special-purpose vehicles, ownership intermediaries, regulatory matters and investment-management litigation. Personal civil proceedings and private home or family details are outside scope. Inferent and unresolved corporate candidates are included as conditional research branches, not established affiliations.

Official public records are the evidentiary basis. Third-party material is a lead only. A filing is attributed to its filer; hosting by a regulator does not make every assertion an independently adjudicated fact. The dossier labels official-record inferences and keeps the records that support them visible.

A missing result is described by its actual search scope. NYSCEF remains an accepted access gap. No authenticated PACER Case Locator or CM/ECF search was performed. [LIT-C012](claims.md#c-954cffa933b56cf4) The five prior Library research files were inaccessible and have not been reconciled. Existing legacy repository material was excluded from research by instruction. The [follow-up instructions](../remaining-work-instructions.md) specify what remains needed.

Readers can follow each claim identifier to its supporting records and exact page or field locator. The evidence appendix retains the registered wording and classifications, including earlier formulations that later records qualify. Where an earlier access gap has been resolved, a current-reading note says so.

### Preservation and interpretation
