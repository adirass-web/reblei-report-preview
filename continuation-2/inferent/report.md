## Inference assessment and alternatives

No reliable Inferent-specific inference about Bleich can be drawn from these originals alone. Matching quantitative strategies or compatible dates would not identify an employee. The original August2018 brochure, physicalp4 Item4, introduces “Inferent QE Client, LP” as the “Client,” then refers to “The Master Fund” in the following paragraph. Both the original page rendering and extracted text confirm that wording. The reused original March6,2019 ADV, physicalp19 ScheduleD Section7.B(1), reports INFERENT QE MASTER FUND, LP, privatefundID805-6736389159. This comparison establishes inconsistent wording, not its cause. A drafting error, a name change, different entities or some other explanation remains unresolved; none is selected. The Client wording is retained as a source-label ambiguity and is not promoted to a verified new entity or merged with the Master Fund. See INF3-C008.
