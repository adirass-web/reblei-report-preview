  • Query API
    • Equity
      • Alternative Display Facility
      The Alternative Display Facility (ADF) is an SRO display only facility that is operated by FINRA.
      Blocks Summary
      Aggregated ATS trade data in NMS stocks that meets certain share based and dollar based thresholds.
      Consolidated Short Interest
      The Consolidated Short Interest data provides a single view of OTC short interest positions submissions across all exchanges.
      Monthly Summary
      Monthly aggregate trade data for OTC (non-ATS) trading data for each firm with trade reporting obligations under FINRA rules.
      OTC Block Summary
      Aggregated OTC (Non-ATS) trade data in NMS stocks that meets certain share based and dollar based thresholds.
      OTC Daily List
      OTC Daily List
      Over-the-Counter Reporting Facility
      The OTC Reporting Facility (ORF) is FINRA's service for reporting and disseminating trades in OTC Equity Securities executed off-exchange and trades in Restricted Equity Securities under Rule 144A.
      Reg SHO Daily Short Sale Volume
      Daily short sale aggregated volume by security for all short sale trades executed and reported to a FINRA trade reporting facility.
      Threshold List
      OTC Regulation SHO and Rule 4320 Threshold Securities.
      Weekly Summary
      Weekly aggregate trade data for OTC (ATS and non-ATS) trading data for each ATS/firm with trade reporting obligations under FINRA rules.

      FINRA's current and historical rules and regulations

      Breakdown of securities industry registered firms by type of registration.

      • 4530 Customer Complaints

    • Fixed Income
      • Agency Pass-Thru TBA Pricing
      This dataset covers Single Family 15-year and 30-year pools issued by UMBS (Uniform MBS, the joint Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac security), FHLMC (Freddie Mac), and GNMA (Ginnie Mae).
      Agency CMO Pricing by Deal Vintage
      Provides daily pricing statistics for Agency Collateralized Mortgage Obligations (CMOs).
      Agency Debt Market Breadth
      Market breadth calculations for Agency debt securities.
      Agency Debt Market Sentiment
      Market sentiment calculations for Agency debt securities.
      Agency MBS Trading Activity
      Daily trading activity statistics for agency mortgage-backed securities.
      Agency Pass-Thru ARM and Hybrid MBS Pricing
      Daily pricing statistics for specified Agency Pass-Thru adjustable-rate (ARM) and hybrid mortgage-backed securities.
      Agency Pass-Thru Specified Pool MBS Pricing
      This dataset covers fixed-rate products: Single Family 15-year, Single Family 30-year, and other fixed-rate term variants for UMBS, FHLMC, and GNMA.
      CBO / CDO / CLO Pricing
      Daily pricing statistics for Collateralized Bond Obligations (CBOs), Collateralized Debt Obligations (CDOs), and Collateralized Loan Obligations (CLOs).
      Corporate 144A Debt Market Breadth
      Market breadth calculations for Rule 144A corporate debt securities.
      Corporate 144A Debt Market Sentiment
      Market sentiment calculations for Rule 144A corporate debt securities.
      Corporate And Agency Capped Volume
      Capped volume calculations for corporate and agency debt.
      Corporate Debt Market Breadth
      Market breadth calculations for corporate debt securities.
      Corporate Debt Market Sentiment
      Market sentiment calculations for corporate debt securities.
      Daily CMBS Pricing by Deal Vintage
      Provides daily pricing statistics for Commercial Mortgage-Backed Securities (CMBS).
      Non-Agency CMO and ABS Pricing by Product
      Provides daily pricing statistics for Non-Agency CMOs and Asset-Backed Securities (ABS).
      Non-Agency CMO Pricing by Deal Vintage
      Provides daily pricing statistics for Non-Agency CMO P&amp;I securities broken down by the era in which the underlying deals were originated
      Securitized Product Capped Volume
      Capped volume calculations for securitized products.
      Securitized Products Data Errata
      provides a log of corrections to previously published securitized products data.
      Securitized Products Trading Activity - Non-Agency and CMBS
      Daily trading activity statistics for securitized products other than agency pass-thru MBS.
      TRACE
      Trade Reporting and Compliance Engine (TRACE)
      Treasury Daily Aggregates
      Daily trading volume in US Treasury Securities reported to TRACE.
      Treasury Monthly Aggregates
      Monthly trading volume in US Treasury Securities reported to TRACE.
      Weekly CMBS Pricing by Deal Vintage
      Provides weekly pricing statistics for Agency CMBS, aggregated across a full Monday-to-Friday trading week.

      Composite Individual Seed

      FinPro Tasks
      Enable FIRM users to automatically retrieve all FINPRO tasks/alerts for their registered representatives

      Individual Pre-Registration Search

  • Submission API
    • Registration
      • BR
      BR submission type is for submitting BR filing. Form BR establishes registration of a branch office with FINRA, the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and states that require branch registration.
      Create Individual
      The Create Individual submission type is used to create an initial registration record for an individual that includes the generation of a CRD number.
      NRF
      NRF submission type is for submitting Non Registered Fingerprint information to FINRA.
      U4
      U4 submission type is for submitting U4 filing. Form U4 establishes registration of representatives of broker-dealers, investment advisers with appropriate jurisdictions and/or SROs.
      U5
      U5 submission type is for submitting Form U5 filing. The Form U5 is the Uniform Termination Notice for Securities Industry Registration.
