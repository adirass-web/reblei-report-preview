RELIEF REQUESTED 
 
In the Statement of Claim, Claimant requested (1) damages for breach of the Clearing 
Agreement of not less than $237,594.00; (2) consequential and incidental damages 
stemming from Respondent’s breach of contract to be determined at the hearing but in 
an amount no less than $500,000.00; (3) in the alternative, return of Claimant’s personal 
property, namely $237,549.00 that was wrongfully converted by Respondent; (4) any 
amount owed to Scout for overcharges of any kind, and which is revealed through 
accounting process; (5) interest at the legal rate of 9% from the date of the breach until 
any arbitration award rendered herein is fully paid; (6) Claimant’s reasonable attorneys’ 
fees; (7) cost of arbitration including expert witness fees, filing fees, and hearing cost 
and (8) such further and other relief as the arbitration panel may deem just and proper. 
 
 
In the Statement of Answer and Counterclaim, Respondent requested (1) for all of 
Scout’s claims to be dismissed in their entirety; (2) judgment against Scout for 
Wedbush’s loss of over $675,000.00 in connection with the agreed settlement with 
FINRA, and for Wedbush ‘s attorney fees and cost of approximately $900,000.00; (3) for 
attorneys’ fees, cost and fines incurred in connection with the enforcement of 
Wedbush’s rights under the Clearing Agreement and PAIB Agreement, including but not 
limited to fees incurred in connection with this action; (4) for pre-judgment interest and 
post-judgment interest; (5) for disgorgement and/or restitution; (6) for cost of suit herein 
incurred; and (6) for such other and further relief as the panel may deem just and 
proper. 
 
In its Answer to Respondent’s Counterclaim, Claimant requested dismissal of 
Respondent’s Counterclaim; reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs incurred; costs of 
arbitration including expert witness fees, filing fees, and hearing costs; and such further 
and other relief as the Arbitration Panel may deem just and proper.  
 
OTHER ISSUES CONSIDERED AND DECIDED 
 
The Arbitrators acknowledge that they have each read the pleadings and other 
materials filed by the parties.   
 
On or about October 6, 2016, Claimant filed a Motion for Sanctions. Respondent

The parties have agreed that the Award in this matter may be executed in counterpart

AWARD 
 
After considering the pleadings, the testimony and evidence presented at the hearing, 
the Panel has decided in full and final resolution of the issues submitted for 
determination as follows:   
 
1. Respondent is liable for and shall pay to Claimant the sum of $237,594.00 in 
compensatory damages. 
 
2. Respondent is liable for and shall pay to Claimant interest on the above-
stated sum at the rate of 4.5% per annum from August 19, 2014 through and 
including the date of the award, plus interest at the rate of 9% per annum 
from the date of the award until paid in full. 
 
3. Respondent is liable for and shall pay to Claimant $1,250.00 to reimburse 
Claimant for the non-refundable portion of the filing fee.  
 
4. Respondent’s Counterclaim is denied.  
 
5. Any and all relief not specifically addressed herein, including attorneys’ fees, is 
denied.
