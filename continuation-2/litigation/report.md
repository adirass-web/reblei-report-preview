# Investment-management litigation: new arbitration evidence

FINRA's native Arbitration Awards Online search located an original award in **Scout Trading, LLC v. Wedbush Securities Inc., case 15-02816**. This is a trading/clearing-agreement dispute between firms, within the professional-conduct scope. It is not a personal civil proceeding or an investor complaint against Bleich.

The award identifies its parties by name, without Scout's CRD or Bleich's name. The connection to Scout CRD151990 is a **probable inference from multiple official records**, not a unique-identifier match: the exact name, Wedbush counterparty and clearing relationship, and the counterclaim's USD675,000 regulatory-settlement context align with Scout's firm report, the FINRA disciplinary bulletin and the newly retrieved Wedbush AWC. A name-collision alternative remains possible. See LIT3-C10.

## Award outcome and limits

The [original FINRA award](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/aao_documents/15-02816.pdf), physical page3, orders Wedbush to pay Scout:

- **USD237,594 compensatory damages**;
- interest at **4.5% per annum from August19,2014 through the date of the award**, then **9% per annum from the date of the award until paid**;
- **USD1,250** reimbursement of the nonrefundable filing fee.

Wedbush's counterclaim is denied. Other relief not specifically granted, including attorneys' fees, is denied. The panel separately assesses USD9,550 in hearing-session fees to Wedbush, payable to FINRA. The record establishes payment obligations; no collection, satisfaction or later court enforcement was verified. No interest total is calculated. See LIT3-C04/C05/C06/C09.

The pleaded causes were breach of contract, conversion and accounting. The award does not explain which cause supports its damages decision. Its claims summary is not an adjudication of every pleaded allegation. Wedbush's counterclaim denial does not vacate or reverse regulatory findings or fines.

## Dates and printed amount difference

FINRA's database labels November18,2016 as the award date, and the signature counterparts explicitly stamp that date as **Date of Service**. The arbitrators' signatures are dated November16. The document's interest provision uses **date of the award**, so this investigation does not decide whether that legal boundary is the signature or service date. The seven physical PDF pages comprise substantive Award pages1-4 and three signature counterparts of Award page5. See LIT3-C07.

The relief-requested section prints USD237,594 for clearing-agreement damages but USD237,549 for the alternative conversion/property request. The panel awards USD237,594. Both requested figures are preserved without a silent typographical correction. See LIT3-C08.

## Separate regulatory proceeding corroborated

The [original Nasdaq Wedbush AWC20100243866-02](https://www.nasdaqtrader.com/content/marketregulation/NASDAQ/DisciplinaryActions/WEDB_NQ_2016.pdf) was accepted February25,2016. It identifies Wedbush CRD877 and names Scout as its clearing client (physical pp3-4). It imposes censure, a **USD675,000 joint Nasdaq/FINRA fine**, including USD337,500 payable to Nasdaq, and a supervisory-procedure revision undertaking requiring registered-principal attestation within 60 business days of acceptance (p8). Wedbush consented without admitting or denying the findings, before a hearing and without adjudication. The sanctions-effective date was to be set separately by FINRA staff (p9). Acceptance, sanctions-effective date, payment and undertaking completion are different facts; completion/payment were not established.

This original strengthens the already indexed Wedbush event. It does not create an additional USD675,000 sanction, add that fine to Scout or Bleich, or alter Scout's separate USD3million action. The award's Member vs. Member category does not establish FINRA membership: the FINRA bulletin expressly describes Scout as a Nasdaq member, not a FINRA member.
